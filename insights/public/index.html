About and privacy

Each morning the living-room TV sends one summary of the day before: when the screen was on, which apps were open and for how long, and how fast its home screen opened. Nothing about what was watched, and no names.

It is kept privately on Vercel, for this app only. This page keeps the last copy on your phone so it opens offline, and one cookie keeps you signed in for a year. No analytics. The fonts come from Google Fonts.

Energy is an estimate: hours on times a typical 95 W, not a meter reading. The cost uses about ₹6.70 a unit (BESCOM's flat rate plus surcharges); the real figure from the bill replaces it.
