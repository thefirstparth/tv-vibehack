About and privacy

Each morning the living-room TV sends one summary of the day before. It holds: when the screen was on; which apps were open, when and for how long; which TV input (such as the Xbox) was picked on the home screen's Inputs menu, and for how long; how fast its home screen opened; how often the sport and weather came in late; restarts, free memory and storage, Wi-Fi drops; which version of the home screen it runs; and any trouble it had sending. Nothing about what was watched, and no people's names.

Every summary is kept privately on Vercel (in Mumbai) for this app only, and copied weekly to Parth's computer. Nothing is deleted. This page keeps the last copy on your phone so it opens offline, and one cookie keeps you signed in for a year. No analytics. The fonts come from Google Fonts.

Energy is an estimate: hours on times a typical 95 W, not a meter reading. The cost uses about ₹6.70 a unit (1 unit = 1 kWh).
