# `cmd package set-home-activity --user 0 com.gathin.tvhome/.MainActivity` first.
# setupwraith must go too: its RecoveryActivity (HOME priority 1) otherwise wins over launcher/ and
# Home shows a black screen, and the shell cannot disable just that component. It is the Google TV
